Weight:

- OTA with diagonal and eyepiece = 0.5 kg (Sarblue Mak60 Review)

Models:

- Sarblue:
  - Comes with erecting 90 degree diagonal and 20 mm eyepiece
- Acuter Maksy
  - Comes with erecting 90 degree diagonal and 20 mm eyepiece
  - Different tripod and smartphone adapter

Mounting:

- Has three ¼-20 threaded holes on the mounting plate.

Focal length is 750 mm, so with 32 mm Plössl eyepiece the field will be 2.1 degrees. However, this will be vignetted by the 0.965 inch port, perhaps in the outer 10% of the field.

Maximum used magnification (at 30×/inch) is 72× or (at 50×/inch) or 120×. This will be achieved with a 10 mm eyepiece or a 6 mm eyepiece.

- C90 is MXP 6200 on Amazon, including a 32 mm eyepiece.
- Cost of 60 mm Mak \+ 32 mm eyepiece is 5200\. Only save MXP 1000\.

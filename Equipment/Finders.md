# Finders

## The “Initial Pointing” Problem

I have discovered that sighting along the tube of my 70 mm SFLR does not give me sufficient accuracy to reliably get a target in the 3.8&deg; field of the Omni 32 mm eyepiece or the 3.6&deg; field of the X-Cel LX 25 mm eyepiece.

My evaluation is that I need a more accurate means for initial pointing, one that allows me to get the Moon, a planet, or a bright star within the field of the main telescope. Once I have that, I can star-hop using the main telescope.

I decided I either needed iron sights, a laser, or a red-dot sight.

## Green Laser Pointer (GLP)

From my birthday in 2026, Caro and the kids bought me a green laser pointer and mount:

- [POGEPE green laser pointer](https://www.amazon.com.mx/dp/B0DFH46HKQ) (MXP 189)
- [Astromania dovetail base](https://www.amazon.com.mx/dp/B01EOET7DS) (MXP 372)
- [Astromania laster pointer mounting bracket](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DK2ZGNWM) (USD 20)

The laser is rechargeable via a micro USB port.

In August and September 2026, I tested it at SPM with my 70 mm SFLR and Noémie's C90. I found it made it very easy to get to the initial bright star for subsequent star hopping. I feel it has solved the difficulties I have had in the past with initial pointing.

Noémie does not trust this laser to be eye-safe. Therefore, in September 2026 I replaced it with a class 3R laser bought from Laser Classroom, a reputable dealer in the USA.

- [Classroom Green Laser Pointer (Class 3R)](https://laserclassroom.com/products/classroom-green-laser-pointer) (USD 40)

This laser is powered by two AAA batteries.

## Conventional Finders

I cannot use a GLP during massive events like the Noche de Estrellas. There are also some risks to using it in Tlalpan, given that aircraft landing at the Mexico City airport from the south fly overhead.

One safer possibility is a conventional finder. Since I will star-hop with the main telescope, I do not think I need a large finder, just one with enough grasp to find bright stars and enough field to find them reliably. One possibility is:

- [SVBONY SV182 6 &times; 30 mm RACI finder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09BQCVXKV) (USD 50 or MXP 1300).

This is a RACI finder with a 7.5&deg; field. It weighs only 290 g, and this is important for a small main telescope. The optics have good reviews, but the mounting is considered to be poor. This aspect could probably be improved by using conventional rings, possibly the ones shared with my GLP.

Apparently, sometimes the eyepiece lenses are incorrectly oriented, but this can be [fixed](https://www.cloudynights.com/forums/topic/895496-svbony-sv182-6x30-raci-finderscope-how-to-fix/).

I bought this on 27 September 2026.

# Celestron Omni 32 mm Plössl Eyepiece

## Field

The Celestron page states that the apparent field is 44 degrees and the focal length is 32 mm. The box states it is 50 degrees.

Unscrewing the barrel of the eyepiece gives access to the field stop, and I measure its diameter to be about 26 mm.
Like most 1.25-in eyepiece barrels, it has an internal M28.5×0.75 threads for filters with a clear diameter of about 27.7 mm (i.e., the barrel walls intrude by about 2 mm in radius).

[Discussion of the field on Cloudy Nights forum](https://www.cloudynights.com/topic/668532-afov-of-32mm-celestron-omni-plossl/).
The conclusion is that it is about 50 degrees.

The Celestron page states that the apparent field of the 40 mm is 43 degrees. This corresponds to a field stop diameter of 31.5 mm. This seems unlikely, given that the outer diameter of the barrel is 31.75 mm and, as stated above, the clear diameter of the filter threads is 27.7 mm. For 27.7 mm, the calculated apparent field is 38 degrees.

## Poor Image Quality

It can give a large exit pupil, so lack of sharpness or aberrations might be the eye.

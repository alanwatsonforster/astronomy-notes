# Equipment List

## Telescopes

My main telescope is a SVBONY SV503 70ED 70mm f/6 refractor. The lens is a doublet with one S-FPL51 element and another of unknown glass. It accepts 2-inch and 1.25 inch eyepieces. The focuser has a smooth action and a 1:10 fine adjustment. It is compact and light.

I've also used in the past a Suncatcher 70 mm f/6, made largely from plastic. I stopped using this once I got a solar filter for the SV503.

## Eyepieces and Diagonal

I am currently using a Celestron Omni 32 mm, Celestron E-Lux 25 mm, and Celestron Omni 15 mm eyepieces with a Celestron Omni 2× Barlow lens. These are all Plössl designs with 50-degree apparent fields.

The magnifications, fields (in degrees), and drift times (in minutes) with the SV503 are:

|Eyepiece|Magnification|Field|Drift Time|
|---|---|---|---|
|32 mm|13×|3.8|7.6|
|25 mm|17×|3.0|6.0|
|15 mm|28×|1.8|3.6|
|25 mm with 2×|34×|1.5|3.0|
|15 mm with 2×|56×|0.9|1.8|

The drift time is the time for an equatorial object to drive halfway across the field.

I'm using a Celestron diagonal.

I have considered a 40 mm Omni eyepiece, but I don't think it would give a much wider field. My understanding is that its field of view is limited by the tube diameter, which corresponds to 4.3 degrees on the SV503. (The actual field will probably a little less, since this field is for a diameter of 1.25 inches and the eyepiece walls will presumably reduce the physical field stop diameter slightly below this.)

The Celestron Omni series also has 4, 9, and 12 mm eyepieces, but these have low eye relief. Thus, with these eyepieces I am limited to a magnification of about 50×.

A possible improvement would be to use X-Cel eyepieces with the corresponding diagonal and Barlow. These have a 60 degree apparent field, so the 25 mm would have a field of 3.6 degrees and could replace the 32 mm. The eye relief is maintained even in the eyepieces with smaller focal lengths, so this gives the possibility of achieving more magnification.

I used to use a Celestron 8–24 mm zoom eyepiece, but it started to give double images.

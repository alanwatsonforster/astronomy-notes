# Celestron Omni 2× Barlow Lens

The total length is about 74 mm.

The original telescope focal plane is 36 mm from the front of the barrel.

The lens is close to the front end, inset by about 2 mm, and is about 6 mm thick. So, the midpoint of the lens is about 5 mm from the front of the barrel.

Assuming the midpoint of the lens is 69 mm from the final focal plane and 31 mm from the original focal plane, the focal length is about -56 mm.

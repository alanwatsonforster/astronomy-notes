# Eyes

## 2022-01-21

I had Lasik surgery on both eyes to correct about 2 diopters of myopia and significant astigmatism.

## 2022-02-18

On my second night with the SV70 telescope, I discovered astigmatism in my right eye. I noted this in my log book.

## 2022-03-29

I investigated the presence of astigmatism as a function of exit pupil diameter for both eyes using the SV70 telescope, Celestron Omni 32 mm eyepiece, and Celestron 8–24 mm zoom eyepiece. Reproducing my notes from my log book, I found:

|*f*|EP|Left|Right|
|--|--|--|--|
|32 mm|5.3 mm|Yes|Yes|
|24 mm|4.0 mm|Yes but less|Yes|
|18 mm|3.0 mm|Yes but still less|Yes but less|
|12 mm|2.0 mm|No|Yes but still less. Messy.|
|8 mm|1.3 mm|No|No. Messy.|

By “messy” I mean that the intra- and extra-focal images were not disks, but showed structure. The astigmatism in the left eye was always less than in the right.

I conclude that both of my eyes have some astigmatism with a 5 mm exit pupil, with more in the right than the left, but the left cleans up nicely for an exit pupil of 2 mm or less whereas the right stays messy.

I also notice that in daytime viewing of distant objects, my right eye is not as sharp as my left. This would appear to be consistent with some residual astigmatism in my right eye.

## 2022-04-02

Appointment with Dr. Naranjo.

In the distant reading test, my left eye performed better than my right. With corrective lenses, the right improved to the level of the left.

He measured 0.25 diopters of astigmatism in my right eye and 0 in my left eye, confirming my qualitative estimates.

I asked him how large the corrected area was; he replied 6.8 mm in diameter.

He measured my pupil in the dim light of his office to be 4 mm. His office was still much brighter than at night.

# Traveling

There are three scenarios:

- Looking after my stuff at home. I need to keep the telescope, accessories, logs, and atlases safe, but the tripod, and stool are assembled.
- Traveling by car. I need to safely transport the telescope and accessories, but the tripod and strip can travel loose.
- Traveling by plane. In this case, I will need to check the tripod, stool, and pan-tilt heads.

Suitcase

I have an AmazonBasics 20-inch Carry-On suitcase.

- My tripod only fits diagonally.
- My stool doesn’t fit.

I’ll probably need to check the tripod and stool anyway, so it might just be worth getting a similar 26-inch suitcase for $1400.

The tripod comes with a soft case. With the tripod in the case:

- The stool fits more or less but sticks out.
- The pan-tilt heads don’t fit

The telescope came with a foam case that, reduced in height for the telescope without rings, is 38 cm long, 21 cm wide, and about 13 cm height. I bought a Truper 16-inch case.

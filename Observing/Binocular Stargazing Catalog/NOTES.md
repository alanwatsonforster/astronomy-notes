# Binocular Stargazing Catalog

The Binocular Stargazing Catalog (BSC) was compiled by Tom Price-Nicholson and lists 150 objects that are visible in 7×50 binoculars. It was published by Sky & Telescope in 2016.

Of the 150, all are Messier or Caldwell objects except the LMC (BSC 2), SMC (BSC 8), and NGC 1502 (BSC 71).

The initials of the catalog clash with the [Bright Star Catalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_Star_Catalogue), and this could provoke some confusion. However, objects in the Binocular Stargazing Catalog are referred to as “BSC” followed by a number whereas stars in the Bright Star Catalog are typically referred to by “HR” (“Harvard Revised”) followed by a number.

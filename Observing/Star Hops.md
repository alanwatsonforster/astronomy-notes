# Star Hops

## Messier Objects

- M34
  - Start at β Per (Algol).
  - Go north about 2 degrees, halfway to κ Per.
  - Go east about 4 degrees to M34.
- M37 = U26
  - Start at β Tau (Elnath).
  - Go west about 6 deg to 136 Tau.
  - Go north about 5 degrees to M37.
- M38 = U22
  - Start at β Tau (Elnath).
  - Go north about 7 deg to M38.
- M41 = U33
  - Start at α CMa (Sirius).
  - Go south about 5 deg to M41.
- M50 = U35
  - Start at α CMa (Sirius).
  - Go east about 2 deg to ι CMA.
  - Go north about 5 deg to θ CMa, passing μ CMa.
  - Continue north about another 4 deg to an asterism of 3 stars.
  - Go east about 2 deg to M50.

## Caldwell Objects

- C14 = U9/10 = NGC 884/869 = Double Cluster
  - Start at α Per (Mirfak).
  - Go east about 9 degrees to 64/65 And, passing ι Per and θ Per.
  - Go north about 7 degrees to the Double Cluster.
- C50 = U30 = NGC 2244
  - Start at α Ori (Betelgeuse).
  - Go about 2 deg south and then about 7 deg east to 8 Mon.
  - Go about 3 deg east to NGC 2244.
